# Gameplay visual references

Approved direction: A's clear geometric shapes with C's quieter center and
restrained effects. Keep both dark and white themes, readable silhouettes at
actual gameplay size, distinct hazard lanes, and a visible player during clears.

- [A — Dark Radar](a-dark-radar.png): shape and color reference.
- [B — White Precision](b-white-precision.png): light-theme reference.
- [C — Neon Surge](c-neon-surge.png): background and motion reference.

These boards are illustrative concepts. Current screen, actor, pickup, and
weapon-effect snapshots in `TiltArenaTests/__Snapshots__/` are the implemented
visual baseline. The shipped weapon atlas is
[WeaponGlyphs](../../../TiltArena/Resources/Assets.xcassets/WeaponGlyphs.imageset/weapon-glyphs-v2.png).
