# Tilt Arena concept prompts

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. Each direction uses a separate generation call. These are art-direction concepts for review, not exported game sprites.

## a-dark-radar

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: game visual-direction concept board for Tilt Arena, an existing iPhone landscape top-down 2D tilt-controlled survival game.
Create one polished raster concept board, landscape 3:2. The top roughly three quarters is ONE complete 2:1 landscape gameplay mockup; the bottom is a small spacious reference strip with enlarged player, three distinct enemy shapes, and three pickup icons. Clearly separate the reference strip from the gameplay. Flat orthographic top-down camera, no perspective. This is practical small-screen game art, not a cinematic poster.
Game invariants: one small arrowhead player craft with a notched tail, cyan accent and bright core; enemies are small saturated red dots and simple red directional shapes, roughly one third of player diameter. Weapon pickups are circular orbs with bold distinct symbols: amber concentric shockwave, cyan lightning, violet gravity spiral. Stage about 24 enemies in readable chasing clusters and one line formation, with clear open routes around them. Put one desirable orb close to a cluster so risk/reward is visually evident. Include a short curved player trail, a single localized weapon clear, and one dashed red incoming-hazard lane. Player remains identifiable during effects. Scene mostly open space. Keep enemy solidity and pickup hollow outer boundary distinct. The gameplay craft is about 3% of gameplay viewport height. No extra characters, ships, terrain, asteroids, buildings, bosses, inventories, virtual joysticks, health bars or mechanics.
HUD: very slim top row in a crisp readable sans serif, score at left, time near center, compact combo at right; exact text "12,480", "00:38", "x8". Title and specimen labels ONLY as specified below. No paragraphs, marketing claims, logos, watermark or device frame.
Tiny-scale readability: strong silhouettes, broad color blocks, one bold icon per pickup, very little internal detail, no noisy texture; all effects leave hazards legible.
Direction: A — refined dark radar, strongest continuity with the current game.
Scene/backdrop: deep navy almost-black arena, extremely faint sparse grid and a suggestion of low-contrast radar arcs. Thin understated border. Calm empty background.
Style/medium: premium restrained luminous 2D arcade art, crisp flat solid shapes with subtle rim light; no jewel-like rendered stickers. Small arrow craft in near-white with cyan edges and a short elegant tapering trail. Red hazards are solid and crisp. Orbs use just one broad illuminated boundary and a chunky glyph. Amber shockwave is a clean expanding partial ring with a few short impact flecks, never an opaque explosion. UI and world share a restrained geometric design language. No decorative reticles surrounding ordinary enemies or many nested player rings.
Color palette: current deep navy, near-white/cyan player, red threats, amber/cyan/violet pickups.
Text (verbatim): title "A / DARK RADAR"; strip labels "PLAYER", "THREATS", "PICKUPS". Keep title understated outside the mockup.
```
