Tilt Arena full-run decisions — 9 September 2026

**Use 0.3 seconds of post-weapon recovery and 10 points per pickup. Retain the other tested defaults.** The [261-case results](2026-09-09-full-run-results.md) and [raw attachment](2026-09-09-full-run-comparisons.json) include the combined accepted configuration, named `clearingScore`. Rules version 3 resets incompatible development profiles; no migration or old-score preservation was added.

The harness runs the production scene, natural enemy spawning and pickup replenishment, real SpriteKit callbacks and actual scoring. Each run uses isolated stores and a no-op Game Center client. The reference already includes responsive wave fronts and the shorter Power charge. Classic compares three profiles, three pickup policies and three seeds. Redline tests the complete profile with two policies because it has only two pickup slots. Daily compares fresh and complete profiles with the same common arsenal. All cases run at 60 Hz until collision or 180 seconds.

These are deterministic comparison instruments. The controller knows every harmful body's exact location and uses simple repulsion; it cannot learn, deliberately aim Power, or demonstrate physical tilt comfort. It occasionally crosses an orb it meant to bank or park. The raw data records those deviations. Fresh and partial profiles have no Nova, so their banking rows repeat cycling. Redline runs die before collecting Nova; those rows cannot resolve Redline banking strength. Daily profile pairs are deliberately duplicated to check independence and must not be counted as extra independent evidence.

| Question | Decision and evidence | Remaining uncertainty |
|---|---|---|
| Generic immunity | Reduce recovery from 0.5 to 0.3 seconds. Mean protected fraction falls 7.3 percentage points in Classic, 7.6 in Redline and 7.4 in Daily. Collection grace remains 0.2 seconds and projectile travel coverage remains intact. Classic loses 0.69 observed seconds on average; 21/27 pairs are unchanged and six end sooner. | One complete-profile Nova-banking seed loses 17.83 seconds, also repeated in the two equivalent Daily profiles. Cycling remains protected for roughly 67–76% of active time, so protection is still substantial. The shorter deadline deliberately exposes some formerly safe contact. Human rescue readability remains open. |
| Pickup farming versus clearing | Reduce each pickup from 50 to 10 points without changing kill points, multiplier steps or survival points. Under the old scoring, ordinary Classic cycling gets 61–77% of points from pickups. The combined candidate makes kills the largest score category in every tested policy group. Its measured movement, kills, deaths, pickups and family events exactly match the shorter-safety candidate. | A score-aware human may choose a different route; the controller does not react to points. Score still rewards risky collection, but a pickup now equals one unmultiplied kill rather than five. |
| Freeze hold | Keep 12/14.4/16.8 seconds. The 8-second base candidate produces mixed full-run outcomes, with five earlier deaths, three later deaths and 19 unchanged runs; its single-run time changes range from −62.70 to +46.38 seconds. The original encounter comparison also found lost rescues. | Freeze's improved front changes its utility. Deliberate shatter/escape comparisons are still needed before declaring its hold ideal. |
| Flame movement | Keep 5× for 0.3 seconds. The 4×/0.22 candidate gives nine earlier, nine later and nine unchanged Classic deaths, with large changes in both directions. A shorter displacement alone does not prove a more controllable landing. | Compare real tilt braking and direction correction after a burst on hardware. |
| Nova banking / parking | Retain 50/75/100% clearing and the weighted pickup supply. Complete-profile cycling lasts longer and scores more than either policy in these scripts. Banking does collect overcharged Novas, so it is exercised rather than merely named. | The parking policy intentionally cycles preferred rescue tools and can accidentally collect parked orbs. It does not exhaust every possible optimization; no universal dominance claim is supported. |
| Ordinary combo window | Keep 1.2 seconds. The 1.6-second candidate adds 3,237 points per Classic run on average without changing any measured gameplay outcome. It rewards the same existing script more without evidence of better intentional connections. | Test whether people can deliberately connect clears under the existing window. Do not infer usability from the bot's timer independence. |
| Synergy / cascade score | Keep celebrations score-neutral. +25/+75 shadow awards add points to the same runs; the report quantifies their share under the final score scale. There is no demonstrated need for another score source after fixing pickup dominance. | No actual bonus-driven human strategy was tested. The shadow calculation is not a reward experiment and does not close that usability question. |
| Earlier Classic roles | Retain 30/50/70/95/120/150-second gates. The corrected 20/40/60/80/100/120 candidate cuts mean observed time by 18.32 seconds, with 20 earlier deaths and six later deaths. It still exposes hunters in only 12/27 runs and paddles in 4/27, exactly the reference counts. | The candidate increases pressure without demonstrating wider role exposure. First-exposure comprehension and human run-length distributions remain required. |
| Progression and Daily | Retain the 1,500/3,000 score weapon gates, 5,000 Classic Redline gate, kill/combo gates, and complete-arsenal Daily gate for this pass. All three fresh-profile cycling seeds still pass the score gates with 10-point pickups. Common-arsenal Daily results are identical across personal profiles. | This establishes reachability for the controller, not runs-to-unlock for beginners. No participant-delay evidence supports removing the Daily gate yet. The explicitly Classic 100,000-point achievement remains a long-run mastery target, not an onboarding gate. |

The lower pickup award and shorter recovery were compared together in all modes. Lowering points changed only score accounting in the paired results; it did not silently change spawning or in-run unlocks. Progression is applied when a run ends. The final default values live in source and the tuning catalog reads them; no persisted developer override is required.

**Review corrections before acceptance.** The first pacing trial changed time gates without enabling formations during warmup or arrows during pressure. Those results were discarded. The final candidate adds a sparse 10-second warmup formation interval and pressure arrows at interval 10, speed 150 and count 2. A regression verifies each role is eligible at its advertised gate. Parking now retains its two remembered IDs when the cycling slot is empty, and both incidental parked pickups and Nova charge tiers are exported. Family counters are fed by actual qualified activation events, not individual kills, and clear on restart. Earlier encounter harness values are pinned so adopting new defaults does not rewrite historical comparisons.

Reproduce and validate:

```sh
TEST_RUNNER_TILT_FULL_RUN_SWEEP=1 xcodebuild \
  -project TiltArena.xcodeproj -scheme TiltArena -configuration Debug \
  -sdk iphonesimulator \
  -destination 'platform=iOS Simulator,id=AA706A81-B950-477D-8FAB-B8836EBD57E0,arch=arm64' \
  -derivedDataPath build/DerivedData CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO \
  -only-testing:TiltArenaTests/ArenaFullRunBalanceTests test
```

Export `ArenaFullRunBalanceTests/testExportFullRunComparisons()` with `xcrun xcresulttool export attachments`, then run `scripts/summarize-full-run-balance <attachment.json>`. The validator rejects missing/duplicate cases, nonfinite or negative measurements, impossible exposure, score/tier discrepancies, Daily profile dependence, and gameplay changes in score-only comparisons. Three seeds and one controller are sufficient to identify these accounting and candidate tradeoffs, not to certify balance for people.

Physical-phone acceptance and human progression, aiming, readability and control comparisons remain in the [next acceptance pass](2026-09-09-device-acceptance.md). No score submissions, account configuration, upload or release was performed.
