Tilt Arena next acceptance pass — 9 September 2026

**The next decisive evidence must come from physical phones and people.** The local environment currently reports no available iPhone. Simulator regression, deterministic balance runs and inspected captures are recorded separately and do not close this checklist. Use rules version 3 with source defaults; reset experimental tuning before each standard comparison.

1. **Verify the build and input.** Install a Release build on a small and a large supported iPhone when available. In both landscape orientations, check standard, flat, reclined and custom calibration; sensitivity 0.6, 1.0 and 1.4 must each reach full speed in every direction at an attainable tilt. Check diagonal speed, neutral drift, recentering, background/resume and the first controlled movement after Play Again. Record the actual tap-to-control delay against the intended 0.75-second countdown.
2. **Compare smoothing without other tuning changes.** Counterbalance 0.11 and 0.08 seconds across testers: one starts with each value. Use stop/reverse, narrow-gap and diagonal approach drills, then normal runs. Note overshoot, unwanted jitter, arm/wrist comfort and recovery after Flame. Keep 0.11 unless the alternative improves intentional control without increasing jitter. Simulator keys cannot decide this.
3. **Check rescue and threat readability in both themes at actual size.** Identify collection grace and its expiry, generic immunity versus Razor, freeze versus thaw grace, slowed harmful enemies, all three pickup tiers, Power's moving aim and Lance's first path. Cross a formation through its advertised opening, pass a stationary mine, and track a reflected paddle puck. Check the same distinctions during overlapping waves, Gravity, warnings and mass clears. Confirm pause/resume and death cannot leave a protected-looking craft that is actually vulnerable.
4. **Play paired weapon and policy comparisons.** Alternate immediate cycling, deliberate Nova banking and preferred-weapon parking with complete profiles. Compare Freeze hold only while holding its responsive front fixed. Compare Flame's original and shorter burst with matched approach intentions. Use aiming lanes and corners for Power/Lance and incoming enemies for Gravity. Collect run summaries and note whether deaths were understandable; last pickup is an association, not a causal attribution.
5. **Measure progression with new players.** Start with fresh local profiles; allow a few settling attempts, then record consecutive Classic attempts and unlocks. Keep each participant's order intact. Report per-player run-length distributions, score shares, first/recurring role exposure and runs to each unlock. Compare against provisional 45–90-second settled beginner and 90–180-second practiced targets. Assess whether the complete-arsenal Daily prerequisite delays a participant who already understands the loop before changing it. Verify fresh/partial/full current-schema persistence, independent mode bests and a Daily attempt that crosses local midnight.
6. **Check sustained device performance.** Exercise crowded Classic beyond three minutes and Redline with repeated clears and overlapping effects. Use bounded performance diagnostics for frame time, thermal state and effect/enemy counts. Inspect background/resume, death and repeated retry for retained effects and memory growth. A simulator Release build is build evidence only; do not substitute its speed for device frame pacing.

Keep family events celebratory and graze scoring absent during these checks. Stable glyphs, charge-tier shapes, existing aim feedback and the immunity cue are the starting presentation.
